Five ways of working with numbers

Fast calculation is the visible result. The real work is underneath, and each method trains something different.

1
Abacus (Soroban)

Moving beads in the right order builds accuracy, precision and a feel for numbers.

2
Finger Abacus

Showing numbers on the fingers as they are called out trains reaction time and coordination.

3
Visual Abacus

With eyes closed, children picture the beads and calculate in the mind. This is the best studied part of mental abacus.

4
Vision Abacus

With eyes open, children hold an imagined abacus steady in front of them. It builds sustained concentration.

5
Screen Abacus

Numbers flash on screen and children learn to read them as whole patterns, quickly.
