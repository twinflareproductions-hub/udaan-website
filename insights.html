    QUESTIONS

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Will it add pressure?

    ×

    We run 5 days a week, but classes are short, playful and focused - 40 minutes. Daily touch builds habit, not pressure - like brushing teeth. Most practice happens in class, not as heavy homework. Small batches, visual-auditory-kinesthetic rotation every 12 minutes, so children stay engaged, not exhausted.

    My child is shy — will abacus help?

    +

    Visualization practice builds confidence through repetition, not performance pressure. Many parents notice more comfortable class participation within a few months. Small 1:8 batches ensure every child is seen.

    How is online attention maintained?

    +

    8-max batches, camera-on, bead-cam for finger position, live error correction. Same ISO 9001:2015 certified systems, same faculty, online or flagship centre Indore.

    At what age should my child start?

    +

    5-14 years is the ideal window. Early years build finger gnosis and number sense; later years strengthen working memory and mental visualization for competitive exams.
