Grounded in Science

Grounded in Neuroscience & Cognitive Science

Our teaching methodology isn't just traditional; it is backed by decades of global research in child psychology and cognitive neuroscience.

Dual-Coding Learning

Beyond verbal and symbolic math: Most classroom math relies on verbal and symbolic processing alone. By converting abstract numbers into physical, spatial bead movements, our program adds a visuospatial channel—giving children a second, complementary way to represent and manipulate numbers in their mind, alongside the logic and language skills school already builds.

The Power of Anzan (Mental Abacus)

As children transition from physical abacus boards to Anzan, they visualize the Soroban within their mind's eye. A five-year controlled study published in the Journal of Neuroscience found that long-term abacus-based mental calculation training was associated with measurably enhanced visuospatial working memory, with corresponding changes in brain activation patterns.
Wang, C. et al. (2019). J Neurosci, 39(33), 6439–6448.

Building Calculation Confidence

Researchers describe "cognitive offloading" as our tendency to lean on external tools—calculators, phones, notes—to reduce mental effort. Abacus and mental math training works in the opposite direction: children practice holding and solving problems entirely in their mind, building genuine calculation confidence that doesn't depend on having a device at hand.
Risko, E.F. & Gilbert, S.J. (2016). Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 20(9), 676–688.

The Science of Intelligence

The Science of Intelligence: Elevating Your Child's IQ

A common misconception is that IQ is fixed at birth or simply measures how many facts a child knows. Modern intelligence testing proves otherwise: IQ measures raw cognitive processing power—and that processing power can be systematically trained.

Global standardized IQ tests (such as the Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children and Stanford-Binet) measure four core pillars of brain performance. The Udaan Abacus program directly upgrades three of these foundational pillars.

How Each Pillar Transforms Your Child's Brain

1

Expanding Working Memory Capacity

Working Memory Index

How IQ Measures It: Working memory is your child's mental scratchpad—the number of items they can hold and process in their mind simultaneously.

How Abacus Upgrades It: The average human memory can hold only a limited number of items at a time. By transforming abstract numbers into structured spatial bead patterns, Abacus Mental Computation (AMC) expands this boundary. Our students learn to effortlessly hold and manipulate many digits at once—both forward and backward—because they are reading a vivid mental map rather than reciting numbers.

2

Accelerating Neural Processing Speed

Processing Speed Index

How IQ Measures It: Tracks how fast and accurately the brain decodes, evaluates, and responds to incoming visual data.

How Abacus Upgrades It: High-frequency tactile training on the Soroban builds rapid automation through repetition and practice. The result: calculation latency drops dramatically, so your child can evaluate non-verbal data with remarkable speed and precision.

3

Elevating Spatial & Visual Logic

Perceptual Reasoning Index

How IQ Measures It: Tests a child's ability to analyze visual patterns, recognize complex relationships, and solve non-verbal problems fluidly.

How Abacus Upgrades It: Transitioning to Anzan (Mental Abacus) gives children constant practice at spatial visualization. By rotating, moving, and reading imaginary beads in thin air, students build real skill at pattern recognition, spatial translation, and structural problem-solving.
