A craft, not a quick course

Abacus is more than fast sums. Taught well, it helps a child focus, picture numbers in the mind and think with confidence. This programme teaches you how to do that well.

A small live cohort

Six people learn together, live, so every question is answered and every mistake is corrected on the spot.

Nine levels in two months

You move up one level at a time, and you pay one level at a time.

Beginners welcome

You do not need to know abacus. You need patience, basic everyday English and a real liking for children.

What you will learn

You learn the five methods Udaan uses with children, and how to run a class well.

1
Abacus (Soroban)

Moving beads in the right order builds accuracy and a feel for numbers.

2
Finger Abacus

Showing numbers on the fingers trains reaction time and coordination.

3
Visual Abacus

With eyes closed, the child pictures the beads and calculates in the mind.

4
Vision Abacus

With eyes open, the child holds an imagined abacus steady. It builds sustained concentration.

5
Screen Abacus

Numbers flash on screen and the child learns to read them as whole patterns.

You also learn how to manage a batch, how to train children well, how to keep their attention in a live class, and how to keep parents confident.

We do not promise a higher IQ, guaranteed marks or photographic memory. We teach skills through regular practice.

Questions we are asked

Straight answers, before you apply.

Do I need to know abacus already?

No. Complete beginners can join. You start at the first level and learn step by step.

Do I need to be a teacher?

No. What matters is that you enjoy children and are patient.

How good must my English be?

Basic, everyday communication is enough to join the programme. Stronger spoken English matters later if you wish to teach international batches, where timings are flexible and planned around time zones.

Will I get a job with Udaan?

Not automatically. The programme leads to a certificate. Udaan may invite dedicated instructors to teach its batches afterwards, by invitation only and without any guarantee.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop or computer with a camera, a stable internet connection, and time for the live sessions. We confirm the details in your conversation.

What if I decide to stop?

If you stop at the first level (Level 0), 50% of that level's fee is refunded.

How many people are in a cohort?

The first cohort starts on 26 October and has six places.
