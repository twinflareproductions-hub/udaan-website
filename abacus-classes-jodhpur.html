WHAT CHILDREN LEARN

From Abacus to Anzan

Children start with a physical Soroban and progress to Anzan — mental calculation by visualising the abacus. No finger counting, no calculator, just pure mind math. A structured 11-level journey, about 3 months per level.

Questions

Abacus classes in Jodhpur: your questions answered

Where is the Udaan Abacus centre in Jodhpur?

Udaan Abacus Academy | AJS Jodhpur, 1st Phase, 425, Janta Colony, Kudi Bhagtasni Housing Board, Basni, Jodhpur, Rajasthan 342005. It's in Kudi Bhagtasni Housing Board, Basni. Get directions on Google Maps.

Are admissions open at the Jodhpur centre?

Yes, admissions are open. The easiest first step is a free demo class. Fill the form on this page, or call or WhatsApp +91 88277 73391.

How many children are in a batch?

We keep offline batches to a maximum of 20 children.

What age can my child join?

Any age from 5 to 14. We place each child at a level that matches their age and ability.

Is there a free demo class?

Yes. Your child tries a real class and you see the centre and meet the trainers. Book through the form on this page, or call or WhatsApp +91 88277 73391.

What are the class timings and fees?

Both depend on the batch and level, so we share the current timings and fees when you get in touch or visit. A quick call or WhatsApp is the fastest way to get them.

How many levels are there?

There are 11 levels, from Level 0 to Level 10, and each takes about 3 months. Every level ends with an exam and a certificate, and children can earn medals for performance.

Do we need to buy an abacus or books?

No. Once your child is onboarded, the Soroban and study material are covered, with no extra fee for study material at any level.

Is this an official Udaan centre?

Yes. It's an authorised Udaan Abacus Academy centre and follows the same programme, levels and standards as our first and primary centre in Indore.

Can my child learn abacus online instead?

Yes. We also run live online classes, Monday to Friday, in small batches. See our online abacus classes.

Will abacus help my child in school?

It builds calculation speed, concentration and confidence, which can support maths in school. It isn't a guaranteed way to raise marks, and results depend on regular attendance and daily practice.

Prefer to learn from home?

Your child can learn the same programme live online. See our online abacus classes, or visit our first centre: abacus classes in Indore.
